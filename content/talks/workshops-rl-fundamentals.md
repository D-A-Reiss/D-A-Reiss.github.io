In several iterations of this workshop at TNG (more are planned for the future), my TNG colleagues Johannes Flommersfeld, Jonathan du Mesnil de Rochement, Daniel Hirsch, and me gave a theoretical introduction to the concepts behind reinforcement learning (RL) and contrasted that with implementing and training own RL agents with [Stable Baselines3](https://stable-baselines3.readthedocs.io/en/master/) and [Gymnasium](https://gymnasium.farama.org/index.html).
By the end of the workshop, participants had a solid understanding of the theoretical concepts underlying RL and the practical skills to implement an RL training suite in real-world applications.
